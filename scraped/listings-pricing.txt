Why This Matters for Your Business

When a customer reports that a product doesn't match the detail page, Amazon investigates. If the listing images тАФ whether AI-generated or not тАФ misrepresent the product, your listing can be suppressed or removed. This falls under Amazon's misleading detail page policies, and it can result in:


Your listing being hidden from search until a compliant image is submitted

A Product Condition Customer Complaint or Product Authenticity Complaint on your Account Health Dashboard

The need to submit an appeal with documentation, including invoices and a plan of action


The key point: you are responsible for all content on your listing, including anything generated by AI. Whether you used Amazon's built-in AI tools, a third-party generator, or created images manually, the standard is the same тАФ your images must accurately represent the product the customer will receive.



What Amazon's Image Policy Actually Requires

Here's a quick refresher on the image standards that apply regardless of how the image was created:


Main images must have a pure white background (RGB 255, 255, 255), show the actual product, and contain no text, logos, graphics, or watermarks

Images must be high quality тАФ not blurry, pixelated, or misleadingly enhanced

The product should fill at least 85% of the frame

Images must accurately represent the product being sold тАФ no illustrations or renderings that misrepresent what the customer will receive

No promotional text, badges, customer reviews, or warranty information

If your image includes photorealistic AI-generated people, you must tag that image with the appropriate metadata before uploading it to Amazon.


If your images don't comply, Amazon may suppress your listing or remove non-compliant images. You can check your suppression status in the Fix tab within Manage Products.



Practical Tips for Using AI Images Responsibly


Always compare AI output to your actual product тАФ side by side, before publishing. AI tools can subtly alter colors, proportions, textures, and packaging details

Use AI for lifestyle and secondary images, not as a replacement for real product photography on your main image

Review AI-generated images for compliance тАФ the same rules apply. White background, no added text, accurate representation

Keep your original product photos on file тАФ if you ever need to appeal a removal, having real photography as backup documentation is invaluable

Check your listings regularly тАФ quarterly audits of older listings can catch outdated or non-compliant images before a customer complaint does

AI-generated people disclosure - If your image includes photorealistic AI-generated people, you must tag that image with the appropriate metadata before uploading it to Amazon.
