A Serious Concern About the Misuse of Amazon Seller Forums





The Amazon Seller forums were created to provide a space where sellers can ask questions, share experiences, seek guidance, and help one another overcome challenges.



Unfortunately, some members appear to be abusing this platform for purposes other than helping the community.



It is concerning to see members using sellers' genuine requests for assistance as an opportunity to promote themselves as consultants, showcase their supposed expertise, or belittle sellers who are already struggling with difficult situations.



Sellers come to these forums because they need answers, not because they want to be lectured, humiliated, or made to feel inferior.



There is a significant difference between offering constructive advice and exploiting someone's difficult circumstances to advertise services or boost one's own image. Disrespecting sellers who are seeking help does not demonstrate expertise, professionalism, or leadership. It demonstrates a lack of understanding of what a support community should represent.





Amazon should pay closer attention to this behavior and review whether such conduct violates its Seller Forums Community Guidelines or applicable policies regarding promotional activity and respectful participation.Nobody should be made to feel foolish for asking a question or admitting they are struggling.



Every experienced seller was once a beginner, and every successful business has faced challenges. The Seller Forums should be about supporting sellers, not exploiting their problems.



We need more meaningful contributions, constructive guidance, and mutual respectтАФnot self-promotion disguised as assistance or arrogance disguised as expertise.
